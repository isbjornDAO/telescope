# Naming and theme

Isbjørn is Norwegian for polar bear. Iggy is the bear. The world should feel like it belongs to that bear: cold, bright, vast, and alive.

The core objects keep their working names — profile, scout, crew, faction, region, Elder, Anchor, season, Victor — because they are clear. The theme layer sits on top. **(all proposals)**

- Seasons could be named as winters: *Winter I*, *Winter II*, each with its own theme.
- A region's in-person cluster could be a **floe** — the ice the community stands on.
- The trust graph could be rendered as ice: strong links are thick ice, weak links thin, and a slashing event is a crack.
- Standing could be **weight** — what a bear needs to survive the winter. Factions that carry weight into a new season are the ones that last.
- The world map is the Arctic; regions are placed on it; scouts are visibly roaming it.

The one rule for the theme: it must never make the world feel like a charity. The bear is the reason, not the pitch.
