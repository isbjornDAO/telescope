# Profiles and scouts

## The profile

A profile is a pseudonymous identity with three layers:

**What you have shipped.** Imported from Builder's Hub as proofs, not as a link to your Builder's Hub account. The world can see that you shipped, and what, without learning who you are on the other platform.

**Who vouches for you.** Your position in the [trust graph](../trust/trust-graph.md): how many vouches, from which regions, at what weight. Shown as proofs and aggregates, never as a list of names unless the vouchers chose to be visible.

**What you are looking for right now.** A role you want to fill, a partner project you need, a region you want to bring a tool to, a research question you care about. This is the field scouts work from, and it goes stale on purpose: it is tied to seasons, so it has to be renewed.

A profile also carries your faction, your crew, your region, your standing, and your season history.

## The scout

Every profile has a scout. A scout is an agent that:

- holds your private context (skills, intent, location, constraints) in a private state;
- continuously searches the world on your behalf;
- talks to other scouts in their own language, negotiating whether two people, two crews or two regions fit;
- pays other scouts for lookups, introductions and attestations, from a budget you set;
- brings you only the matches worth a human conversation.

Scout-to-scout is where the synchronicity actually happens. Humans meet once the scouts have already agreed there is a fit, and both humans have opted in to disclose.

You can bring your own agent as your scout, as long as it speaks the scout protocol. **(proposal)** Isbjorn ships a default scout so nobody needs to build one to participate.

## Why scouts never sleep

Scouts keep looking between seasons. That is what keeps the network alive when there is no tournament running, and it is the source of the constant activity that justifies Telescope having its own chain. See [Iggy L1](../platform/iggy-l1.md).

## What a scout may reveal

A scout reveals nothing identifying without your consent. It may reveal zero-knowledge proofs about you ("holds at least N vouches from region X", "has shipped in the last two seasons") to other scouts as part of negotiation. Full disclosure — name, contact, history — happens only after a mutual match and only to the extent each side chooses.
