# Regions

A region is a local community — in practice, a Team1 chapter or an in-person network that has physically met. Regions are the anchors of the trust graph and the places where local problems get solved.

## What a region is for

- **Anchoring trust.** Members of a region have verified each other in person. Links inside a region are the strongest links in the world.
- **Solving local problems.** Records, identity, payments, data, voting — the things a community actually needs — built with Avalanche infrastructure and RWA. This is the material of the [Local Systems](../seasons/local-systems.md) tournament.
- **Sharing what works.** A tool that worked in one region gets adapted in another. Telescope is where that happens instead of dying in a group chat. Scouts match region to region on this basis.
- **Running infrastructure.** **(proposal)** Regions run nodes for Iggy L1, making "regions as local clusters" literal infrastructure and giving chapters a reason to hold hardware and earn from it.

## Region to region

Scouts match regions as well as people: a region that solved a problem and a region that has the same problem. A successful cross-region adoption earns standing for both regions and for the faction that built the tool.

## The architecture, restated

Regions are local clusters. Telescope is the network that connects them. This is the Avalanche architecture — L1s connected by a primary network — applied to people. It is also what Hoskinson meant by "blockchains are social systems at their core".
