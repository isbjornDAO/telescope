# Crews and factions

The progression is the game. A person becomes part of a crew; crews become a faction; a faction becomes a lasting organisation.

## Crew

A crew is a small team that ships something. Crews form through scouts, at in-person events, or by invitation. A crew has a name, members, a region of origin, and a record of what it has entered and won. Crews are the unit that enters the [GTM](../seasons/gtm.md) tournament.

## Faction

A faction is the container for "large lasting company". It is more than one crew, held together by a shared vision, with:

- a **vision** — what the faction exists to build (privacy infrastructure, local records systems, climate and conservation tooling, agentic finance, and so on). Factions form around visions, not tokens;
- a **treasury** — allocated from season results and faction dues, governed by the faction's members;
- **presence in more than one region** — a faction that only exists in one place is a crew with ambitions;
- **standing** — accumulated trust weight from seasons, which makes the faction's vouches and votes count for more.

## Alliances

Factions that can only compete turn tribal. Factions that can ally on a shared problem produce the cross-region tool sharing that Telescope exists for. Alliances are first-class: two or more factions can register an alliance for a season, pool a submission in Local Systems or Research Papers, and share the resulting standing according to terms they set in advance. **(proposal)** Alliances cannot pool for GTM, where a clear single Victor matters.

## Becoming lasting

A faction that carries one idea through all three tournaments — researches it, designs the local system for it, ships the product — is the pattern Telescope is built to produce. That arc, across three seasons, is what "large lasting company" means here. See [Seasons overview](../seasons/overview.md).

## Membership rules

A person belongs to one faction at a time. A crew belongs to one faction at a time, or to none. Moving between factions is allowed between seasons, not during. A faction's standing does not travel with people who leave it.
