# Seasons overview

Telescope runs two seasons a year. Each season is six weeks, online-first with optional in-person integration, the same rules and the same timeline in every region. The structure is fixed; the theme changes each season.

Each season runs three tournaments at once:

| Tournament | What it rewards | Who decides | Submissions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Local Systems](local-systems.md) | Design quality and ethics of decentralised initiatives and local community development | A panel of Elders | Public |
| [Research Papers](research-papers.md) | Research that advances the season's research question | Blind review | Private |
| [GTM](gtm.md) | The best dapp on Avalanche this season | Community vote, weighted by trust | Public |

## Three kinds of legitimacy

The three tournaments use three different ways of deciding, and this is deliberate. Each protects against the failure the other two would have:

- The panel in Local Systems resists popularity contests.
- Blind submission in Research Papers resists clout.
- Community vote in GTM provides the spectacle and gives the world a Victor it chose itself.

## The tournaments feed each other

This is the loop that turns factions into lasting organisations:

1. **Season one** — a faction enters a Research Paper on the season's research question.
2. **Season two** — that research becomes the brief for a Local Systems design: how would this actually work for a real community, and is it ethical?
3. **Season three** — the design becomes a GTM build, shipped and put to the community's vote.

A faction that carries one idea through all three has done what a company does — research, design, ship — inside the world, with a public record at every step. That is the pattern Telescope is built to produce, and it is why people stay across seasons.

Nothing forces a faction to follow the arc. A crew can enter GTM cold. But standing compounds fastest for those who do.

## Season structure

- **Week 0** — theme and research question announced. Profiles renew their "looking for" fields. Scouts go to work.
- **Weeks 1–4** — building, writing, designing. Scouts continue matching; alliances register.
- **Week 5** — submissions close. Voting rounds and panel review begin.
- **Week 6** — knockouts, finals, Victor named. Standing and treasury allocated.
- **Between seasons** — scouts keep running. Retention is measured at 90 days.

## Retention is the score that matters

Prizes and support are tied to continued building. The metric the world watches is not how many people signed up for a season but how many are still building 90 days later. Standing from a GTM win is only fully vested once the shipped product has held its users through that window.
