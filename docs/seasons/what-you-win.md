# What you win

The prize that compounds is standing. Everything else is secondary.

## Standing

Standing is season-earned trust. A crew or faction that wins or places gains standing, which raises their trust score. That means their vouches count for more, their votes count for more, and their scouts can make stronger claims in negotiation. Standing vests fully only after the 90-day retention check for GTM, and immediately for Local Systems and Research Papers.

Standing belongs to the crew or faction that earned it and does not travel with people who leave.

## Season title

Each tournament names its finalists and its winner for the season. GTM names the Victor. Titles are permanent entries in the world record.

## Treasury

Faction treasuries receive an allocation from the season pool according to results. Treasury is spent by the faction under its own rules. **(proposal)** The season pool is funded by sponsors and ecosystem programmes, never by Isbjorn's commercial revenue, so that no tournament outcome is ever a question of Isbjorn's interest. See [Economics and the conflict wall](../platform/economics.md).

## The Victor's seat

The GTM Victor represents Avalanche for the season: introductions, marketing, and nomination for ecosystem programmes.

## What you do not win

Tokens are not prizes. Telescope does not run a token sale, does not issue a governance token in its early life, and does not launch tokens for entrants. The path from a winning GTM product to capital runs through the people and funds in the community, off the tournament rails.
