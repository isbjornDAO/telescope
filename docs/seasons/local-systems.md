# Local Systems

**For:** global decentralised initiatives and in-person local community development.

Local Systems is mostly theoretical. Entries are designs, not shipped products: a proposal for how a decentralised system would solve a real problem for a real community — records, identity, payments, data, voting, conservation, whatever the season's theme calls for.

## What is judged

- **Quality of the smart contract design.** Is it sound, safe, minimal, and does it use Avalanche infrastructure and real-world assets well?
- **Ethics.** Who does this serve, who could it harm, what happens when it fails, who holds power over it? Entries are expected to answer these directly.
- **Fit to the community.** Is this what the community actually needs, and could they run it themselves?

## Who decides

A panel of [Elders](../trust/trust-graph.md): verified people with expertise — university teachers, researchers, practitioners — drawn from across regions. Panel composition is published before submissions open. Panelists do not judge entries from their own region or faction.

## Format

Submissions are public. Knockout rounds are decided by panel vote against a threshold (see [Voting and knockouts](voting-and-knockouts.md)). Finalists present to the panel and to the world.

## Why it exists

Local Systems is where non-developers can compete, where the ethics of the world are argued out loud, and where the polar bear ethos lives: the conviction that crypto should solve problems for people and places, not only trade against itself. It is also the middle step of the three-season arc, turning research into something a community could adopt.
