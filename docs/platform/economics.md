# Economics and the conflict wall

Telescope is run by Isbjorn, and Isbjorn is a company. Some profit is to be made. Telescope is also supported by Team1 and touches ecosystem money. Those two facts have to be kept apart by structure, not by good intentions.

## Free for builders, forever

No person, crew or faction ever pays to have a profile, run a scout with the default budget, enter a tournament, or vouch. The people the world exists for do not pay for it.

## Where Isbjorn earns

- **Sponsored seasons and tracks.** Protocols and companies that want builders on their stack sponsor a season theme or a tournament track. This is the ETHGlobal model: builders pay nothing; sponsors pay for access to builders.
- **Recruiting.** When a company hires through Telescope — a crew, a person, a faction's services — Isbjorn takes a placement fee. Matching is free; hiring is where the value is.
- **Premium tooling for funds and sponsors.** Structured deal flow, analytics, scout capacity above the default budget.
- **Scout economy.** A small protocol fee on scout-to-scout payments on Iggy L1.

Isbjorn commits a fixed share of its revenue to polar bear conservation. The share is published, the recipient is named, and the flow is on-chain.

## The conflict wall

Anything that touches Team1 or ecosystem funds is protected from Isbjorn's commercial interest:

- Tournament season pools are funded by sponsors and ecosystem programmes and are allocated by tournament results. Isbjorn takes nothing from them.
- Grant and ecosystem allocations run on Telescope at cost. Allocation decisions sit with a committee Isbjorn does not control.
- Panel composition, review pools and voting thresholds are published before each season and cannot be changed during it.
- Isbjorn staff and their crews may participate in the world but may not enter tournaments in a season Isbjorn sponsors.

This is written down, published, and signed off with Team1 before the first season. A founder who publishes her own conflict rules is a credibility asset; the same facts kept quiet are a liability.

## What Telescope does not do

Telescope does not sell tokens, does not run a token launchpad, and does not issue a governance token in its early life. If a compliant path to capital formation is ever added, it is a separate product with its own legal basis, and the tournament rails stay clear of it.
