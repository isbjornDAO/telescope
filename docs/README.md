# Telescope — World Rules

**Version 0.1 · draft · September 2026**

Telescope is a world, not a social network. It runs on Avalanche and is built by Isbjorn, supported by Team1.

Its job is to help people find their team, find other teams, and connect the regions of the Avalanche network that are solving real problems where they live. Builder's Hub is where you prove what you built. Telescope is where you find who to build it with, and argue about what is worth building.

The holistic goal of Isbjorn is to save the polar bears. Telescope works towards that goal by bringing the ethos of world problem solving back to crypto: a place where privacy, decentralisation, local infrastructure and new smart contract ideas are treated as things to build, not things to trade.

## How the world works, in one paragraph

Every person has a **profile** and a **scout** — an agent that goes looking on their behalf, talks to other scouts, and only brings back matches worth a human conversation. People form **crews** (small teams that ship something) and crews form **factions** (lasting organisations with a shared vision, a treasury, and presence in more than one region). Trust is a **graph of vouches**: in-person communities anchor it, online shared-vision links extend it, and every vouch is a stake. Twice a year the world runs a **season** with three interconnected **tournaments** — Local Systems, Research Papers, and GTM — and what a faction wins is standing that compounds into the next season.

## The non-negotiables

Safety, security, privacy and pseudonymity are the core of the project. Everything in this document is subordinate to them. See [Core principles](principles.md).
