# Telescope — World Rules

**Version 0.1 · draft · September 2026**

Telescope is a world, not a social network. It is built by Isbjorn, supported by Team1. Avalanche is the primary engine that settles work and solves real-world problems; you do not need to care about Avalanche to research or build here.

Its job is to help people find their team, find other teams, and argue about what is worth building for the future of governance, finance, and the planet. Builder's Hub is where you prove what you built. Telescope is where you see what everyone is working on, publish research, and run tournaments that turn papers into tools.

The holistic goal of Isbjorn is to save the polar bears. Missions and tournaments lead to Arctic conservation journeys. Telescope works toward that by treating privacy, decentralisation, local infrastructure and new smart contract ideas as things to build, not things to trade.

## How the world works, in one paragraph

Every person has a **profile** and a **scout** — an agent that goes looking on their behalf, talks to other scouts, and only brings back matches worth a human conversation. People form **crews** and crews form **factions**. Trust is a **graph of vouches**. **Research** runs as continuous bounty events (essay terms, deadlines, prizes) and builds a citable archive. Twice a year the world also runs a **season** with interconnected **tournaments** — Local Systems and GTM — fed by that research. **Projects** shows what everyone is building on a public board. What a faction wins is standing that compounds into the next season.

## The non-negotiables

Safety, security, privacy and pseudonymity are the core of the project. Everything in this document is subordinate to them. See [Core principles](principles.md).
